## Upload to Google

1. Open the **ZIP** or **PPTX** link above on your Mac.
2. Save the file (Downloads folder).
3. Go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com) → **New** → **File upload**.
4. For slides: right-click the `.pptx` → **Open with** → **Google Slides**.
5. For runbook/notes: upload the `.md` files or paste into new Google Docs.
